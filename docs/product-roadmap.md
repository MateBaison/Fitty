# Fitty product decisions — 2026-10-18

Keep the current Home design. No personalized Home redesign and no workout scheduling/reminder feature in this iteration.

Current scope: first-use profile → guided manual/automatic routine creation, selectable country, editable training-frequency suggestions, simpler progress summary, explicit date-period comparison, global support entry, and same-muscle equipment filters for replacement.

Future ideas retained, not implemented here: wearable integration and taking a meal photo to estimate calories and macronutrients. Food estimates would need explicit uncertainty, portion correction and separate provider/privacy evaluation.

Frequency defaults (2 days for beginners/returning, 3 regular, 4 experienced) are conservative product starting points, editable from 1–6 days, not clinical prescriptions. WHO recommends adult muscle strengthening on at least two days/week; it does not establish our experience-specific mapping. Reference: https://www.who.int/europe/news-room/fact-sheets/item/physical-activity . The automatic routine reuses the existing exercise-selection generator; goal is retained as the selected training goal, not a claim of individualized clinical programming.
