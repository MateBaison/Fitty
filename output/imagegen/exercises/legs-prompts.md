Generated with the built-in image_gen tool using the supplied masculine and feminine mannequin references. `legs-manifest.json` stores the common prompt, the 45 exercise-specific prompts, selected generator source paths, and final filenames. Each final is 1536 × 1024, with initial pose on the left, final pose on the right and a thin white divider.

Counterpart instruction: female reference defines identity and intact sports bra/shorts; masculine exercise reference defines matching camera, equipment and movement. Red anatomical muscle fibers remain a translucent overlay over complete clothing, with feathered boundaries.

Floor bridges were regenerated to retain full feet within the frame.
